## Brand & Style

WishDem uses a proprietary midnight-plum, mulberry, porcelain, and champagne visual identity. Editorial Playfair Display carries names and emotional promises while Manrope keeps the service precise and easy. Tactile correspondence materials—paper, seals, ribbons, and glass-light—turn scheduled wishes into future gifts. Dark atmospheric canvases frame warm paper surfaces; motion feels like an object being opened, never like loud celebration.
